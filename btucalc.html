How to measure a room

Floor size

  • Run the tape along the floor from one wall to the wall facing it, then do the same the other way.
  • Include space under beds and cabinets. Skip closets with doors and attached bathrooms.
  • Odd-shaped room (like an L)? Measure it as two boxes and add both areas.
  • Using a tape in feet? Switch the calculator to Feet at the top.

Sun

  • Pick Strong sun when the room heats up in the afternoon: a wall or big window facing west, or a top-floor room right under the roof.

Ceiling

  • Measure from the floor straight up to the ceiling. Most homes are about 2.4–2.9 m; leave the box empty for those.
