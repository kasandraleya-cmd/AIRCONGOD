How pay is worked out: Daily rate × days worked · Per job rate × jobs done · Pay is weekly: Monday to Sunday, paid on that Sunday · Monthly rate × 12 ÷ 52 for a week, minus absent days at monthly rate ÷ 26 · Overtime = hours × (daily rate ÷ 8) × 1.25 · Deductions are typed in as amounts. Rates come from the Staff page and are saved with each period, so later rate changes don't change past payroll.

Teams & incentive

Each day, when a team reaches its DONE unit installed on the Daily Schedule, every member on the crew gets their share below. Counted per unit, not per client (each row's QTY): one client with 2 units = 2 units. A member not on the crew that day (absent) gets nothing, and their share isn't given to anyone: each member present gets only their own share.
(unticked: paid again for every unit after that)
